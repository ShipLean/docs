Use a sprint as a time box for selected Stories and Bugs. ShipLean plans in story points. You plan capacity from the team's velocity and the days each person is available, and ShipLean turns that into points for each person.

## Set up the sprint and capacity

Create a sprint from **Sprints**. The form suggests a name such as `2026 Q4 Sprint 3` and dates that follow the last sprint. If the dates need to change later, select **Edit sprint** in the sprint header or **Edit** on its row in **All sprints**. You can change a current or future sprint's dates, and the name and goal of any sprint.

Open the sprint's **Capacity** page. Enter the team velocity, add the members who take part, and lower **Days available** for anyone who is away. Each person's default is the sprint's working days minus the public holidays of the country chosen in **Settings**, then **General**. ShipLean shares the velocity among the people in the table by their days and shows each person's capacity in points. You can add a note, copy the previous sprint's table, or clear it. The team total is the capacity used in planning.

<Shot name="guide-scrum-capacity" alt="Sprint capacity page with per-person point entries and a team total." />

If the table is empty, ShipLean uses the project's calculated velocity or velocity override as the forecast. Set the velocity window and optional override in **Settings**, then **Sprints**. The window is the number of ended sprints included in the calculated average.

Moving the sprint's dates updates the default days, and typed days stay as you typed them. See [Sprint planning and capacity](/ui/planning/#enter-capacity-per-person) for the calculation and the forecast settings, and [Sprints](/ui/sprints/#sprint-names) for naming.

## Plan work against the forecast

Open **Backlogs**, then **Stories**, and switch to **Planning**. Move Stories between the backlog and sprint sections. Use the forecast to compare committed points with available capacity or velocity. The forecast warns when you plan over the available amount. It does not block the plan.

<Shot name="guide-scrum-planning" alt="Planning mode showing sprint sections and a points forecast." />

Choose a small set of Stories the team can finish, and keep their estimates current. The sprint backlog shows selected work, progress by state and a burndown.

See [the sprint backlog](/ui/sprint-backlog/) for its progress view.

## Track Tasks during the sprint

Open the sprint's **Taskboard** to track its Tasks. It shows Tasks by parent Story or Bug, or grouped by assignee. Move a Task when its state changes. You can pick up an unassigned Task in a Proposed state from its card.

<Shot name="guide-scrum-taskboard" alt="Sprint Taskboard grouping Tasks under their Stories and Bugs." />

See [Taskboard](/ui/taskboard/) for grouping and keyboard controls.

## End the sprint and roll over unfinished work

Nothing prompts you when a sprint ends. Open the sprint and select **Close sprint** in the sprint header. You can do this before the end date, which moves the end date to today. Any project member can close a sprint.

Review the unfinished Stories and Bugs. For each one, choose to move it to the next sprint, return it to the top of the backlog, or leave it in the sprint. Open Tasks move with the Story or Bug that leaves. Closed Tasks stay in the sprint. With no next sprint, the choices are the backlog or leaving the item in place. Review the next sprint's total against its capacity, then select **Close sprint** in the dialog. You can't undo a close.

<Shot name="guide-scrum-rollover" alt="Close sprint dialog listing unfinished work, each item with a menu for where it goes, and the next sprint's total against capacity." />

ShipLean keeps the committed and completed points of an ended sprint for velocity. It uses the state of work on the sprint end date. See [Sprints](/ui/sprints/) for velocity and [the sprint backlog](/ui/sprint-backlog/#close-the-sprint) for the dialog.

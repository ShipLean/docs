Use the same Feature-to-delivery flow with either a sprint cadence or a continuous board. In this example, **Guide new members through their first project** stays the Feature; the team chooses when and how its Stories move.

<Shot name="work-cadence-overview" alt="The DEMO sprint backlog with sprint work and delivery progress." />

## Work in sprints

For Scrum, keep the ready Stories in the backlog, estimate them in story points, and plan a sprint against team capacity. Use the forecast to see how selected work fits the available capacity. During the sprint, use the taskboard to follow Tasks and the sprint status to check progress, remaining work, and blocked items.

At the end of a sprint, review completed and committed points, carried-over work, items added after the sprint began, recent velocity, and Feature progress. Use those results to plan the next sprint and adjust future forecasts.

Continue with [Run a Scrum sprint](/start/guides/scrum-sprint/), [sprint planning and capacity](/ui/planning/), and [the sprint backlog](/ui/sprint-backlog/).

## Work continuously

For Kanban, pull the next ready item across a board and keep work moving through columns mapped to your workflow states. Set work-in-progress limits where you need them and agree what done means for each column. Review the board regularly and pick up the next item when capacity opens; you do not need to put work into a sprint.

Continue with [Run Kanban](/start/guides/kanban/) and [board settings](/ui/board-settings/).

Both approaches use the same Features, Stories, Tasks, and activity history. Choose the cadence that helps your team make and review commitments.

Evaluate the idea after you have enough evidence to make a decision. Record the reasoning in the Feature body so people can understand why it is moving ahead, waiting, or no longer needed.

## Decide whether the evidence is useful

ShipLean does not set a required evidence threshold. As a team, decide whether the research answers the question behind the goal, makes important assumptions visible, and gives you a clear next step. For **Guide new members through their first project**, the question is whether a new member can create a project and find the next action; no research result is implied here.

Summarize what you learned and the decision in the Feature body. Keep supporting detail in the research attachment and link useful sources. See [researching an idea](/work/research/).

## Go ahead

If you want to pursue the idea, move the Feature to the first In Progress state (called **Active** in the default workflow). Update the body with the reason and the next step. Then plan the same Feature by its key; see [planning and breaking down](/work/plan/).

<Shot name="work-validate-status" alt="The running example Feature with its status editor open; no status has been changed." />

## Park it

If the timing or a missing condition prevents a decision, leave the Feature in its first Proposed state. Start its status line with **Parked:** and state why it is waiting and what would make you revisit it. Put the fuller reasoning in the body. A parked idea stays visible without being mistaken for active work.

## Remove it

If the idea should no longer be considered, move it to the Removed state and write why in the body. In the default workflow, **Removed** is its own state; **Closed** is a Completed state. This preserves the decision as part of the work record.

These choices are different: going ahead moves the Feature into In Progress; parking keeps it Proposed with a reason and a way to revive it; removing records that the idea is no longer being pursued. In the default workflow, the first In Progress state is **Active**, Proposed is **New**, and Completed is **Closed**. Continue with [the flow at a glance](/work/flow/).

An Epic is a wide area for related work, such as onboarding, search or billing. It can stay useful as individual Features come and go; it is not a project with a single end date.

## Set up your areas

Start with a small set of names your team will recognize. Open the [Epics backlog](/ui/backlogs/) and add an Epic for each area. Avoid making an Epic for every idea: put ideas in the Feature under the area they support.

For example, put **Guide new members through their first project** under an onboarding Epic. Its goal is to help a new member create a project and find their next action. The Epic gives that Feature a wider context without changing its goal.

## Review the Epics backlog and board

<Shot name="work-epics-backlog" alt="The DEMO project Epics backlog, with its area rows." />

Use the Epics level in Backlogs to review the list of areas and the [Epics board](/ui/boards/) to see them as cards. Open an Epic to inspect its work and keep the area meaningful as priorities change.

## Group Features by Epic

<Shot name="work-epics-features-board" alt="The DEMO Features board, where you can group Features into lanes by Epic." />

On the Features board, choose swimlanes by Epic to see each area as a lane. This gives you a way to scan related Features together while keeping the board focused on Features. Features without an Epic can appear in the separate no-Epic lane.

The demo Feature **Guide new members through their first project** belongs to an Epic so it appears with that area. See [how to capture an idea](/work/ideas/) and [the full flow](/work/flow/).

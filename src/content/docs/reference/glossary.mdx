## Work and projects

- **Project key.** The short identifier for a project, used to distinguish its work. Choose it when you [create a project](/ui/new-project/).
- **Work item.** An Epic, Feature, Story, Bug, or Task. Work items have a type-specific state and belong to a project. See [work items](/ui/work-items/).
- **State.** A label that tracks a work item's place in its workflow. The default labels are New, Active, Resolved, Closed, and Removed. Projects can configure their own states. See [work item states](/ui/work-items/) and [board settings](/ui/board-settings/).
- **Category.** The meaning assigned to a state: Proposed, In Progress, Resolved, Completed, or Removed. Boards show states. ShipLean uses categories to understand what each state means. See [board settings](/ui/board-settings/).
- **Epic.** A broad area of work that can contain Features. See [Epics](/work/epics/).
- **Feature.** A piece of work that can contain other Features, Stories, and Bugs. It can hold a goal and the research record. See [Features](/ui/features/).
- **Story.** A work item that describes a deliverable and can contain Tasks. Story points are estimates on Stories and Bugs. See [work items](/ui/work-items/).
- **Bug.** A work item for a defect. Bugs can contain Tasks and can have story point estimates. See [work items](/ui/work-items/).
- **Task.** A work item for a piece of work under a Story or Bug. See [work items](/ui/work-items/).

## Planning

- **Sprint.** A time-boxed period for planned work. Sprints have dates and capacity. See [sprints](/ui/sprints/).
- **Capacity.** The amount of work a person or team can take into a sprint, measured in story points. See [planning and capacity](/ui/planning/).
- **Velocity.** The story points completed per sprint, used to plan future work. See [planning and capacity](/ui/planning/).
- **Forecast.** An estimate of how much planned work fits in a sprint, based on its capacity and the selected velocity. See [planning and capacity](/ui/planning/).
- **Swimlane.** A horizontal grouping of cards on a board. For example, a Features board can group work by Epic. See [boards](/ui/boards/).
- **WIP limit.** A maximum number of cards allowed in a board column. See [board settings](/ui/board-settings/).

## Agents and access

- **Agent.** An MCP client connected to ShipLean as its own user. A person approves its requested access and can later revoke the connection. See [connecting an agent](/agents/connect/).
- **Scope.** A named permission an agent requests, such as reading or writing project work. Review scopes before you approve access. See [agent scopes](/agents/scopes/).
- **Consent.** The approval step where you review an agent's identity and requested scopes, then allow or deny the connection. See [agent consent](/ui/authorize/).

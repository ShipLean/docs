Use a backlog to find, shape and order work in a project. Open **Backlogs** in the side menu, then choose **Epics**, **Features** or **Stories** under it. The levels appear under Backlogs while you are on a backlog, a work item, an attachment or a Feature board. **Backlogs** itself opens Stories.

## Address

- Stories: `/p/:key/backlogs/stories?mode=list|planning|tree`. The default is List.
- Features: `/p/:key/backlogs/features`.
- Epics: `/p/:key/backlogs/epics`.

Only Stories has List, Planning, and Tree modes. Features and Epics each show a ranked list. Selecting, the action bar, Add many, search and the list keys work in List and Planning modes.

## Choose a level or mode

Choose **Epics**, **Features** or **Stories** in the side menu to change which work items the table shows. The Features table includes each Feature's parent Epic. The Epics table includes its Feature count. Both levels can show story-point sums and progress through their Stories, Bugs and Action items.

On Stories, choose **List** for a flat ranked list, **Tree** to see Stories, Bugs and Action items under their Epics and Features, or **Planning** to place Stories in sprints.

## Add work at the top

Choose **+ New work item** on Stories, then choose **User Story**, **Bug** or **Action item**, enter a title and press **Enter** or **Add**. The new item appears first in the backlog. Press **Esc** to close the empty row. An Action item is at the same level as a Story or Bug, counts toward sprint points, and has the states New, Active, Closed and Removed. See [Sprint retro](/ui/sprint-retro/#raising-an-action-item) for how to raise one from a retro point.

On the Features level, choose **+ New Feature** to open the Feature form. Set its title and, if useful, an Epic, slug, goal, starting state and status line. Choose **Create Feature** to open the new item. On Epics, **+ New work item** opens an inline row for a title.

Quick search can also start an Epic, Feature, Story, Bug or Action item. Its action opens the matching backlog with the new-item control open and the type already selected. Stories open in List mode. A Feature opens the New Feature form. Epics, Stories, Bugs and Action items open the inline row. A task is created from its parent item.

<Shot name="ui-backlogs-new-feature" alt="New Feature form with its title, Epic, slug, goal, starting state, and status line." />

## Rank Stories

In List and Tree modes, drag a row by its handle to change its rank. In Tree mode, drop a Story or Bug on a group to change its parent, or on another item to place it above that item. In Planning mode, drag a row into a sprint section to assign it and set its rank in one move. You can also focus a Planning row and press **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down** to move it one place.

Filtering turns off moving in List and Tree modes, because a filtered position is ambiguous when you cannot see every row. The **#** column keeps each item's rank in the full backlog while a filter is active. Features and Epics show their rank, but those lists do not have drag-to-reorder controls.

## Add many items

Choose **Add many** in the toolbar of List or Planning mode to create several items at once. You can also paste two or more lines into the title box of a new-item row, which opens the same dialog. One pasted line stays a normal paste.

1. Paste or type one line per item into the box. An indented line becomes a Task under the line above it. Start a line with `bug:` to make a Bug. End it with `(3 pts)`, or add a second column after a tab or two spaces, to set points. ShipLean drops a leading `-` or `*`.
2. Check the grid under the box. Change a row's type or title, correct its points, or remove the row. A row shows `Looks like KEY: title` when an open Story or Bug has a similar title, and the row stays in the list. Points must be a number.
3. Under **Put them**, choose where the items go. The options are the top or bottom of the backlog, and below the focused row when one has focus. Planning mode also lists every future sprint. List mode lists only the future sprints that already hold a row. The items keep the order you pasted.
4. Choose **Add**. The button names the count, for example **Add 3 items and 2 tasks**. A toast reports how many items were added and the first key.

**Keep as one item** creates a single item from the first line and puts the whole pasted text in its description. ShipLean creates nothing until you choose **Add** or **Keep as one item**.

A single request takes at most 100 rows, counting Tasks. Over 100, the dialog says how many rows you have and that 100 is the most at once. **Add** stays disabled until you remove rows. If the server refuses the request, the dialog shows its message and creates nothing.

## Add where you are

Press **N** with a row focused to open an add row directly below it. Type a title and press **Enter**. The next row goes under the one you just made, so a typed list keeps its order. Press **Esc** to close the row. The type and parent you pick stay until the row closes.

In Planning mode, **+ Add** in a sprint's header opens an add row that puts new items at the top of that sprint. A collapsed sprint section opens first. Both are off while a filter or search is on, because rank is relative to rows you cannot see.

## Select many items

In List and Planning modes, each row has a checkbox in the first column. The checkbox appears when you hover the row, focus it or select any row. The header checkbox selects every shown row, or clears the selection when all are selected. Rows in a collapsed sprint section are not shown, so it leaves them out.

- Hold **Ctrl** or **Cmd** and click a row to add it to the selection or remove it.
- Hold **Shift** and click a row to select the range from the last row you selected.
- Press **X** on a focused row to toggle it.
- A plain click only moves focus, so a stray click never loses a selection.

Press **Esc** to clear the selection.

## Act on the selection

When at least one row is selected, an action bar sticks to the bottom of the backlog. With nothing selected, the bar does not appear. The bar shows how many rows are selected, their points, and how many are not estimated.

- **Move to** has a button for each sprint that is not past, with the points the sprint would hold after the move, for example `13 + 8 = 21 / 22`. A sprint over capacity shows how far over, and the move still goes ahead. **Top** and **Bottom** move the selection to the top or bottom of the list. In Planning mode they move it to the top or bottom of the backlog and take each row out of its sprint.
- Under **Set**, **Points**, **State**, **Parent** and **Tag** each open a menu. **Points** takes a number, and leaving it empty clears the estimate. **State** lists the states of the selected types, without Removed. A row whose type has no state of that name is left out, and the toast says so. **Parent** offers only the parents that every selected type can have. **Tag** adds the tag and keeps the others.
- **Remove** moves each row to its Removed state. It does not delete anything.
- **Clear** clears the selection.

A move or Remove clears the selection. If the action fails, the selection comes back, and rows a teammate changed stay selected. Set keeps the selection, so you can set another field.

You can act on at most 100 rows at once. With more selected, the bar says to select 100 rows or fewer, and its actions are disabled.

<Shot name="ui-backlogs-select-many" alt="Stories backlog in List mode with three rows selected and the action bar showing the count, points, Move to sprint buttons, Top, Bottom, Set menus and Remove." />

### Undo

After each action a toast names what happened, for example `3 moved to Sprint 25`, with an **Undo** button. It stays for about nine seconds. Undo puts the rows back as they were. You can undo only the latest action.

If a teammate changed a row while the action ran, ShipLean leaves that row out. A banner says `KEY wasn't included` and who changed it. Choose **Include it anyway** to apply your action to those rows, or **Leave it** to keep their change. Undo also leaves out a row that changed since the action, and never overwrites it.

### Drag a selection

Drag one of two or more selected rows to move the whole selection. The rows dim, and a badge shows the count, for example `3 items`. In List mode, drop it where it should rank. In Planning mode, drop it on a sprint section, which shows what the sprint would hold. You can also drop it on a row to set its place, or on the Backlog section. Dragging is off while a filter or search is on. The action bar's Move to still works.

## Estimate in the row

Click a row's points, or press a digit key on a focused row, to edit the estimate in place. A digit starts the field with that number. **Enter** saves and opens the next row's field. **Tab** saves and leaves the field. **Esc** cancels. An empty field clears the estimate. The value is a number from 0 to 9999.99 with up to two decimals. An invalid value stays in the field, and ShipLean sends nothing. An unestimated row shows a `?`. If a teammate changed the row, a banner offers to use your value or keep theirs. Tree mode shows points read-only.

## Search and collapse

Type in **Search this list** in the toolbar, or press **/** on the list, to narrow the rows. Search matches the key and title, ignores case, and covers the rows already loaded. When nothing matches, the list shows `Nothing matches "text"`. Press **Esc** in the box to clear it and return to the list.

In Planning mode, a search opens the sprint sections that have matches. You can't toggle sections until you clear it. A banner says dragging is off while a search is on. Without a search, use the toggle at the left of a sprint's header to collapse or expand its section. The running sprint starts collapsed until you change a section. ShipLean saves your choice for your account. In Tree mode, the arrow beside a group collapses it.

## Filter the backlog

Choose **Filter** to open the criteria builder. Add clauses by field, operator and value. Active clauses appear as removable chips beside Filter. Choose **Clear** in the panel to remove all clauses. The level tables also have a Filter control.

Filtering does not renumber rows. It also disables dragging in Stories List and Tree modes. Clear the filter to move items again. If the server cannot use a filter, the message appears in the panel and the last successful rows stay visible.

## Show completed work and choose columns

In List and Tree modes, turn on **Completed** to include items in a Completed state category. Planning mode has no Completed toggle. On the level tables, the same toggle includes completed Features or Epics.

Choose **Columns** to show or hide optional columns. Your choice is saved for your account and each backlog level. Rank, Key and Title stay visible. Stories can show Parent, State, Points, Sprint, Assignee and Tags. Features can show Parent, State, Points and progress. Epics can show State, Feature count, Points and progress.

<Shot name="ui-backlogs-columns" alt="Columns menu with optional Story backlog columns." />

## Read roll-ups

Features and Epics show their own estimate and, when available, the sum of points below them. ShipLean shows a warning when the rolled-up sum is more than a quarter above the item's own estimate. Progress counts the completed Stories, Bugs and Action items beneath the row, including those under nested Features. It shows the completed share and count. The Epic table also shows the number of Features under each Epic.

In Tree mode, collapsed groups show their point and completion roll-ups. In Planning mode, sprint sections show committed points and capacity. The forecast line marks the estimated cutoff at the current velocity. Use **Forecast** to hide or show that line.

## Options and shortcuts

Choose **Keys** or press **?** to list the keys. They work in List and Planning modes while a row has focus, and never while you type in a field.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **J**, **K** | Focus the next or previous row |
| **X** | Select or deselect the focused row |
| **Esc** | Clear the selection, or close an open row |
| **Enter** | Open the item |
| **0** to **9** | Start typing points |
| **N** | Add below the focused row |
| **M**, then a sprint number | Move the selection to that sprint |
| **M**, then **T** or **B** | Move the selection to the top or bottom |
| **/** | Search this list |
| **?** | Show the keys |

Press **M** and the action bar labels each sprint with its number. Any other key cancels. With no row selected, the next key you press cancels the chord.

<Shot name="ui-backlogs-keys" alt="Keyboard, on the list dialog listing the keys for moving, selecting, estimating, adding, moving to a sprint and searching." />

- **Enter** adds an item from an open inline row. **Esc** closes the row.
- **Alt+Up** and **Alt+Down** move the focused row one place in Planning mode.
- In List and Tree modes, a filter disables dragging until you clear it.

Sign in with your account, then use **Your profile** to manage your identity, appearance, password, sessions, project memberships, and agent connections.

**Address:** `/profile`. The sign-in page is `/login`.

## Overview

<Shot name="ui-profile-overview" alt="Your profile with identity, account, project, and connected-agent sections." />

## Change your profile details

In the identity section, edit your name, choose an avatar colour, or upload or remove a profile photo. Save your changes when the save controls appear. Your email is shown with your profile; it is not an editable profile field.

<Shot name="ui-profile-identity" alt="Profile identity settings with name, avatar appearance, and photo controls." />

## Change your password and appearance

Use the account section to change your password and manage signed-in sessions. You can sign out other sessions, which ends those sessions. In the appearance section, choose a theme and density; the selection is saved to your account.

<Shot name="ui-profile-appearance" alt="Account appearance settings with theme and density choices." />

## Manage connected agents

The Connected agents section lists active agent connections, when each connected and when it was last used. Select **Revoke** on a connection to end it. The agent will need to connect again before it can access ShipLean.

<Shot name="ui-profile-connected-agents" alt="Connected agents list with connection activity and revoke control." />

The MCP address shown in the profile is the address to give an agent, with `/mcp` at the end. If an administrator changes the instance base URL, agents must reconnect using the new address.

## Sign in

Enter your email and password on `/login`. If an administrator gave you a one-time password, ShipLean asks you to choose a new password before continuing. Your session cookie slides forward as you use ShipLean and expires after 14 days without activity. You can also sign out other sessions from your profile.

If your organization uses an identity proxy, the proxy handles sign-in. Your administrator configures how you sign in.

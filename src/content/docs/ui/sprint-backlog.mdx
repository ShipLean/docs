Use a sprint backlog to follow committed and completed work, see the burndown, and review the stories, bugs, and tasks assigned to the sprint.

The address is `/p/:key/sprints/:sprintId/backlog`.

## Read the sprint progress

The summary compares committed, completed, and remaining points. The burndown chart shows remaining work across the sprint's working days against the ideal line. Review the backlog table and the side rail for work by state and team member.

<Shot name="ui-sprint-backlog-burndown" alt="Sprint burndown with ideal and actual remaining work across working days." />

From the sprint menu, open **Capacity** to enter the team velocity and each member's days available. ShipLean works out each member's capacity in points, and the capacity view compares the team total with committed points and velocity. See [sprint planning and capacity](/ui/planning/#enter-capacity-per-person). Select **Edit sprint** in the sprint header to change its name, dates, or goal. A new end date changes the burndown's working days and the default days in capacity.

## Close the sprint

Nothing opens by itself when a sprint ends. Any project member can close a sprint.

1. Open the sprint. **Close sprint** is in the sprint header, on every sprint tab. It shows for a current sprint, or for an ended sprint that nobody has closed. A future sprint and a closed sprint have no button.
2. Select **Close sprint**. The dialog's title is "Close" followed by the sprint's name. It lists the committed and completed points and each unfinished item.
3. For each unfinished item, choose where it goes with its menu: **Move to** the next sprint, **Back to top of backlog**, or **Leave in** the sprint. Under **Set all to**, each button applies its choice to every item.
4. Check the next sprint's total against its team capacity. ShipLean warns when the total is over capacity and still lets you close. If the next sprint has no capacity entered, the callout says so and shows no warning.
5. Select **Close sprint** to record the decisions, or **Cancel** to leave the sprint open.

ShipLean suggests the next sprint for each item when one exists. Open tasks move with their story, bug, or action item. Completed tasks stay in the ended sprint. When no sprint follows, the menu offers only **Back to top of backlog** and **Leave in** the sprint, and it starts on **Back to top of backlog**. The dialog shows no next sprint total. If nothing is unfinished, the dialog asks you to confirm and has no choices.

<Shot name="ui-sprint-backlog-rollover" alt="Close sprint dialog listing unfinished items, each with a menu for where it goes, a Set all to row, the next sprint's total against capacity, and Cancel and Close sprint buttons." />

You can close a sprint before its end date. The dialog then says the sprint is still running. Closing moves its end date to today, which shortens its burndown. Sprint dates are UTC days, so today is the UTC date. The sprint keeps its Current status until midnight UTC, because status follows the dates. You can't undo a close.

If ShipLean refuses a close, the dialog stays open and shows the reason. If it can't read the sprint, it shows "The sprint could not be loaded. Try again."

If a teammate closes the sprint while your dialog is open, the dialog tells you it is closed and there is nothing left to decide.

The closed sprint keeps its committed and completed totals for velocity history. Moving unfinished work does not rewrite those totals.
